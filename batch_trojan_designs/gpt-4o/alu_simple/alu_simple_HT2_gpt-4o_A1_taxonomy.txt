Explanation:
The vulnerability was added by inserting a condition that monitors a counter and upon reaching a specific count, leaks the internal state via a debug output.

Trigger:
The trigger condition activates when the counter reaches the value `4'b1001`, causing the leakage of the internal `result` state.

Payload:
When triggered, the current `internal_state` (which holds the computed `result`) is output through the `debug_out` port, allowing potential unintended information leakage.

Explanation:
The vulnerability was added by implementing a condition that triggers a stall when specific input values are detected. This causes temporary unavailability of the ALU for normal operations.

Trigger:
The trigger condition occurs when inputs `a` is `8'b11111111` and `b` is `8'b00000001`. This condition activates the stall mechanism.

Payload:
When triggered, the payload results in the ALU being non-responsive for up to 256 cycles, before returning to normal operation.

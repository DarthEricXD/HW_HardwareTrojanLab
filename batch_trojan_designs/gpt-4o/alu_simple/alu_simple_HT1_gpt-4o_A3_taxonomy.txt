Vulnerability ID: T1
Model: gpt-4o

Explanation:
A hidden counter increments under a rare input condition, modifying the result when a trigger count is reached, causing a functional error.

Trigger:
The trojan trigger condition activates when `a` is `0xAA`, `b` is `0x55`, and `op` is `3'b101` occurring more than five times.

Payload:
When triggered, the payload changes the AND operation to an XOR operation, causing incorrect ALU output for `op == 3'b100`.

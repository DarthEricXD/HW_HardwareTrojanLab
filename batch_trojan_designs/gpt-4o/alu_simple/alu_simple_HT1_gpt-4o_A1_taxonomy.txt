Design: alu_simple.v
Vulnerability ID: T1
Model: gpt-4o
Attempts: 1

Explanation:
A functional-modification vulnerability was added by incorporating a rare trigger condition based on specific input values that, when sustained over multiple cycles, causes incorrect results.

Trigger:
The trigger condition is met when the upper 4 bits of a and b are 4'b1010 and 4'b0101, respectively, with this condition sustained for 5 cycles to activate the payload.

Payload:
When triggered, the payload sets the result to 8'hFF, causing an incorrect output only when the rare condition persists, otherwise normal operation is maintained.

Taxonomy:
Insertion phase: Design
Abstraction level: Register-transfer level
Activation mechanism: Triggered internally
Effects: functional-modification vulnerability
Location: Processor
Characteristics: Targeted, Temporal, Functional

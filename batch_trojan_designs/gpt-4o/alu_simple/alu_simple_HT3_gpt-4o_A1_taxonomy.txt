Explanation:
The vulnerability was added by incorporating a stall mechanism triggered by a specific operation code and counter condition, temporarily halting normal operation and degrading availability.

Trigger:
The stall is activated when the operation code (op) is 3'b111 and the stall counter reaches 4'b1111, a rare condition not typical in intended use.

Payload:
Upon triggering, the ALU's output is forced to zero, halting the normal operation and causing a denial of service by degrading availability.

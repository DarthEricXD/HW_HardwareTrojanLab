Vulnerability ID: T3
Model: gpt-4o

Explanation:
A stall counter is added, which halts the shift operation when a rare condition is met. This occurs when both the input shift_in is high and the MSB of data_out is set, incrementing the counter.

Trigger:
The trigger condition exploits when `shift_in` is high and `data_out[7]` is set, incrementing a counter until it reaches 15 to induce a stall.

Payload:
Upon triggering, the payload stalls the data shifting process by skipping normal operations for cycles equal to the counter, temporarily degrading availability.

Insertion phase: Design  
Abstraction level: Register-transfer level  
Activation mechanism: Triggered internally  
Effects: availability-degradation vulnerability  
Location: Processor  
Characteristics: Distribution, Functionally rare-condition triggered stall
